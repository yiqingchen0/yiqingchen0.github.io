Full project title: *Causal survival mediation modeling to understand the
impact of radiation-induced immunosuppression on cancer survivorship.*

## Approach

- Decomposed the causal relationship between radiation modality and overall
  survival into a direct effect and an indirect effect mediated by
  radiation-induced lymphopenia, to compare the prognostic value of both
  biomarkers.
- Analyzed radiation-induced lymphocyte kinetics in patients with esophageal
  cancer using tumor growth dynamics (TGD) models.
- Improved the lymphocyte kinetics model with quadratic Bayesian hierarchical
  regression.

## Findings

Radiation-induced lymphopenia is a causal mediator of survival after
chemoradiation therapy for esophageal cancer (*Advances in Radiation
Oncology*, 2024). The work was presented at the AAPM 65th Annual Meeting and
the Stat4Onc Annual Symposium in 2023.
