Full project title: *Developing the algorithm of nucleosome position score
system and identifying the positioned nucleosome arrays (NucArray).*

## Work

- Took part in further development of the algorithm from Zhang Y, et al.
  Canonical nucleosome organization at promoters forms during genome
  activation. *Genome Research* 2014;24(2):260–6.
- Developed the algorithm that identifies the nucleosome position score level
  from differential equations.

## Findings

Nucleosome positions are sometimes stable next to biologically significant
regions such as transcription factor binding sites, although most of the
genome is covered by an aperiodic nucleosome signal.
