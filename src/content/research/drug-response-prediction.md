Full project title: *Cancer patient drug response prediction algorithm
comparison and development.*

## Approach

- Developed statistical, machine-learning (random forest, k-nearest neighbors,
  support vector machines and others) and neural-network (CNN, TabNet and
  others) methods using whole-exome sequencing data (GDSC, TCGA) to predict
  response to more than 100 drugs in patients with a variety of cancers.
- Compared 15 methods for predicting patient drug response from baseline gene
  expression and in vitro drug sensitivity in cell lines.
- Built comparison and validation pipelines covering statistical,
  computational and clinical aspects, using cross-validation, simulation and
  real-world clinical cohorts.
