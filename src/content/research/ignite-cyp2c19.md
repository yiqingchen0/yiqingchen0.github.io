Full project title: *IGNITE CYP2C19-clopidogrel expansion project.*

## Question

Does the CYP2C19\*17 allele change clopidogrel's clinical effectiveness and
bleeding risk after percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI), relative to
alternative antiplatelet therapy (prasugrel or ticagrelor)?

## Data

A large multicenter, real-world cohort of patients receiving genotype-guided
antiplatelet therapy in the NIH IGNITE network (Implementing Genomics in
Practice), with sites including UNC, UAB, UPenn, UIC, UPitt, UMaryland and
UIndiana.
