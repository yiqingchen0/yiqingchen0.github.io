## Approach

- A comprehensive investigation of overall survival (OS) between treatment
  arms, using parametric informative Bayesian methods and simulations for
  low-event-rate survival data with crossed survival curves.
- Bayesian model averaging and Bayesian joint modeling to account for
  uncertainty about the data-generating process and time-dependent variables.
- Simulated trial data to study the behaviour of the Bayesian
  hyperparameters.

## Findings

A consistent OS benefit for the active arm compared with placebo in longer
follow-up (after 30 months), and a trend toward an OS benefit for the active
arm among patients with one prior line of therapy.

Presented as an oral presentation and a poster at the 2023 meeting of the
American Statistical Association New Jersey and Princeton-Trenton Chapters.
