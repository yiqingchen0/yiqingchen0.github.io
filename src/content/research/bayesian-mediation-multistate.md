Full project title: *Bayesian mediation multi-state model with application to
radiation-induced cancer progression–death process and trial endpoints
prediction.*

## Approach

A causal mediation multi-state model with Bayesian model averaging (BMA),
built within a stochastic counterfactual framework for cancer
progression–death processes. It combines two estimators:

- Bayesian estimation with multiplicative proportional transition intensities
  (MPTI);
- survival random forest, TabNet and a time-series transformer.

## What it provides

- Treatment effects quantified and visualized as longitudinal counterfactual
  mediation survival risk ratios, for binary, continuous or time-to-event
  mediators.
- Individualized predictions of interim and end-of-trial endpoint
  probabilities, a hedge against imprecise pretrial design assumptions.
- An application in esophageal cancer: the mediating effect of radiation
  modality on overall survival through radiation-induced immunosuppression, in
  a longitudinal setting.
