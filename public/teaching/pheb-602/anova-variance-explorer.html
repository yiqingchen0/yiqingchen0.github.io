Why does ANOVA compare variances?

Explore how differences between group means compete with random variability within groups—and why the F test compares both.

Between-group mean square signal + noise—
MSbetween = SSbetween / 2
Within-group mean square noise—
MSwithin = SSwithin / 15
ANOVA F statistic—
F = MSbetween / MSwithin
p-value ——
Two-sided omnibus hypothesis · α = .05

2. Corresponding group distributions LIVE

Bell curves fitted using each group's current sample mean and sample SD. Wider + flatter = larger variance.

Interpretation: These are illustrative fitted normal curves using the observed mean and SD of each group, not a histogram or proof of normality. The ANOVA statistics are calculated directly from the underlying observations.

🧪 Experiment A — Keep the means apart

Hold between-group separation constant. Increase within-group spread. The distributions widen and overlap more; the F statistic falls and the p-value typically increases.

🧪 Experiment B — Keep the noise fixed

Hold within-group spread constant. Increase between-group separation. The centers move apart; the F statistic rises and the p-value decreases.
